sup

i once stated na hindi ako ‘yung tipo na nag su-sulat ng letter and that i don’t think kaya ko mag plan and effort like how the others do it. kasi i once said, i wasn’t that romantic. well here i am, sa’yo to. una ‘to. and tamad ako mag sulat kaya sa’yo na rin ang first coding project ko.

i initially planned to give you glasses, kasi ‘yun ‘yung na-notice ko na super kailangan mo. ako kasi i’m a needs over wants kapag nag re-regalo. kaya nung sinabi mo na bibili ka sa sarili mo, nainis talaga ako nun. kasi naka plano na ‘yun sa’kin eh. kaya ‘yun back to square one, wala akong maregalo nanaman.

tapos dumaan sa tiktok ko ‘yung five senses gift. a gift that should and would (!) tickle your five senses. and here’s what i thought:

sight, the first thing you saw when you opened this paper bag was this black bag. why a bag? kasi lagi ka nag co-complain about the tote bag na ginagamit mo and ‘yung last na black bag na gamit mo is kay bau. kaya ‘yan, a bag for you. ang liit noh? sign na kasi ‘yan, matuto ka na ‘wag mag over pack. ang dami mo mag dala ng gamit! may mga damit pa, pack lightly lang!

hearing, i thought really hard on this kasi nga napaka predictable ng earphones or anything like that. and then i saw this, a mini scannable keychain! ito na ang babakod sa uplb! may picture pa natin (which you said is your favorite). and scannable ‘yang spotify code niyan!

smell, ito it wasn’t that hard kahit nasabi ko na nabilhan na kita ng pabango—bibilhan kita ulit kasi why not. hahaha in all honesty i just wanted to give you more perfume, kasi there’s no harm in having a collection!

taste, obviously, baker ang girlfriend mo! ito baked cookies, which you once bashed! unforgettable moment, “medyo matamis”. BAKA COOKIE TALAGA ‘YAN.

touch, this one i thought really hard on kasi gusto ko nga may matchy tayo. at first i thought i’ll buy a matching shirt or blouse kaso i thought against it. kaya ‘yan. i wanted to give you a watch as a reminder—that i’ll always have and will make time for you. (at dahil one of the chinese superstitions ay nag si-signify na countdown daw ‘to, bigyan mo’ko ng piso para ma-break ‘yung bad omen).

i hoped i showed you how much i love you through everything. i hope you never doubt, but when in doubt let’s not approach others. rather, let’s talk about it with each other.

the following days, months, and years will be hard as we tackle and grow from a distance. through this i hope you never forget that we may be far by kilometers, but we will always be closer in heart and by love.

mahal kita, jem.
